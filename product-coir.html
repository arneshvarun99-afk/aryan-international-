Where we source

02 · Quality
Quality gate

Coir Board publishes product-specific quality specifications covering items such as retted and brown fibre, yarn and mats. For fibre, checks can include moisture, impurities, grade and fibre-length distribution. For some coir products, BIS specifications also set measurable requirements such as mass, thickness, moisture and impurity limits.
