01 · Source
Where we source

Erode Manjal (Erode Turmeric) is a registered Geographical Indication in India. The official GI register identifies it as an agricultural good from Tamil Nadu, with the GI registration valid through 2031. For any GI-labelled commercial lot, we should verify the source and applicable authorized-user / GI documentation before using the GI claim in an offer.

02 · Quality
Quality gate

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University guidance for turmeric highlights buyer-visible checks such as bright yellow colour, suitable finger size, hardness, smooth skin and absence of impurities. For spice exports, analytical testing can be matched to the buyer's specification and importing-country rules; the Spices Board's quality laboratories perform testing to applicable standards and food regulations.
